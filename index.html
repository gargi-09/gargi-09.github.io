About

Reasoning from failure modes

Frontier models are probabilistic. Production infrastructure can't be. My work sits in the gap between the two: the checks, contracts and enforcement points that decide whether a model's output is allowed to touch a real system.

I start from what breaks, how it breaks, and what has to be true for it not to. Then I build the deterministic layer that makes that true, and makes it obvious when it isn't.
